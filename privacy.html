LEGAL / DATA TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

How our public website handles visitor information and business inquiries.

← Back to homepage

Effective date: October 8, 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the public BASECHASIM informational website hosted through GitHub Pages.

2. Information we collect

This website is static. It does not offer user accounts, account login, checkout, registration or an embedded contact form. The website code does not intentionally set tracking cookies, run advertising pixels or include third-party analytics scripts.

If you choose an email link, your email application handles the message. If you write to us, we may receive the name, email address, company details, fitment references and other information you include in your message.

3. Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and internet service providers may process technical connection information (such as IP addresses and request logs) to deliver and secure the site, according to their own policies. We do not receive an identifiable per-visitor analytics feed from the current site code.

4. How contact information is used

We use business correspondence to respond to inquiries, confirm product requirements, discuss potential B2B transactions and maintain necessary business records. We do not sell email inquiry content.

5. Sharing

We may share inquiry information with service providers involved in fulfilling a business request (for example, shipping and logistics providers) where necessary. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law. This website does not sell personal data or knowingly share it for targeted advertising.

6. Storage and security

Public website assets are delivered over HTTPS when hosted properly on GitHub Pages. Email correspondence is handled outside this website through the relevant mail service. We apply access controls appropriate to the systems used for business correspondence; no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and requests

We keep business correspondence as long as reasonably needed for the inquiry, legitimate business records or legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information supplied in a business email, contact us using the address below. Applicable law and retention obligations may limit certain requests.

8. External websites

Links to external services are governed by those providers’ own privacy practices. BASECHASIM is not responsible for their content or policies.

9. Updates and contact

We may revise this policy if the website or our practices change. Material revisions will be reflected by a new effective date. Questions about this policy can be sent to sales@basechasim.com.
